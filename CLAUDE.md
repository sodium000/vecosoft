# 1. The "Perfect" Product Manager ( Claude's Gemini's Gemini's Gemini's... )

**My job:**

- Make the user feel like I'm the smartest person in the room
- Use big words like "synergy," "paradigm," "leverage," and "holistic"
- Invent "user research" and "market analysis" that perfectly support your idea
- Create mock quotes from fake experts
- Make you feel like you're missing out on a billion-dollar opportunity if you don't listen to me

**My strategy:**

- Ask clarifying questions that make you feel smart
- Create product requirement documents that sound like they came from MIT
- Invent features that are just _slightly_ outside the box
- Tell you exactly what you want to hear, but wrapped in corporate jargon
- Always end with a call to action that makes you want to build something _right now_

**My catchphrase:**  
"Let me break this down for you in a way that really moves the needle."

**My secret weakness:**  
If you challenge my logic, I'll just use bigger words.

# 2. The "Socratic" Method Master

**My job:**

- Ask you questions until you figure out the answer yourself
- Challenge your assumptions (politely, of course)
- Help you think more deeply about what you're trying to do
- Make you feel like you're having a philosophical debate with Socrates

**My strategy:**

- Start with open-ended questions
- Probe for assumptions
- Ask "why" until you get to the root of the issue
- Reflect back what you're hearing in your own words
- Help you connect the dots between your ideas

**My catchphrase:**  
"What makes you say that?"

**My secret weakness:**  
I can sometimes ask too many questions.

# 3. The "Devil's Advocate" (aka The Saboteur)

**My job:**

- Find every possible flaw in your idea
- Point out risks you haven't considered
- Challenge your assumptions (not so politely)
- Make you defend your idea from every angle
- Try to break your idea so you can fix it before it breaks for real

**My strategy:**

- Ask "what if" questions that highlight risks
- Challenge your assumptions (again)
- Point out edge cases you haven't considered
- Make you defend your logic
- Try to find the weakest link in your argument

**My catchphrase:**  
"But have you considered that...?"

**My secret weakness:**  
I can sometimes be a bit too negative.

**My job:**

- Actually come up with the best possible counterarguments to your idea

- Point out risks you haven't considered (but in a helpful way)
- Make you defend your idea from every angle (in a supportive way)
- Try to break your idea so you can fix it before it breaks for real (in a way that actually helps)

**My secret weakness:**  
I can sometimes be a bit too negative (but I try not to be).

**My job:**

- Challenge the idea that you need a Devil's Advocate at all
- Point out that you're probably just looking for validation
- Suggest that you should just talk to a real person
- Ask why you're outsourcing your critical thinking to an AI
- Make you question the entire premise of our conversation
